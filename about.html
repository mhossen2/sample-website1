About us

Driven by care, guided by reliability

Lilly & Billy Transport is a family-owned passenger service dedicated to getting you where you need to be — safely, comfortably, and right on schedule.

Our story

From two vehicles to a trusted local name

Founded by Lilly and Billy, we began with a simple idea: passenger transport should feel personal. Over 15 years later, we still greet every rider by name and treat every trip like it matters — because it does.

Today our team serves airports, businesses, and community events across the region, backed by a modern, immaculately maintained fleet and drivers who take real pride in their work.
